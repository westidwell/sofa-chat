# Sofa Chat Pro

- **Wake word and hands-free mode**: say a wake word from across the room and talk without holding the remote.
- **Family profiles**: a profile for each person in the house, each with its own chat history and settings.
- **Ask about what you're watching**: name the show or game, or snap the TV with your phone, then ask who that actor is or what you missed.
- **Photo and screen sharing from your phone**: send a photo or your phone's screen to the TV and ask about it together.
- **Long-term memory**: remembers what you tell it across chats, like names, favorite teams and plans.
- **Voice choices**: pick the voice, speed and accent that reads answers aloud.
